How Seemara builds a custom AI system, step by step

MM
Mamta MorFounder, Seemara · 24 November 2026 · 5 min read
In short

Seemara, founded by Mamta Mor, builds custom AI in four steps: map one workflow and its success number, build a small working prototype, connect it to the tools your team already uses, then improve it with real feedback. You see something working early, before anyone commits to anything complicated.

People often expect an AI project to begin with a long discovery phase and end, months later, with a big launch. Ours run the other way round: small, early and real. Here is the process we follow at Seemara, step by step.

Step 1: Map the workflow

We start with one workflow and how it actually happens today, not how the process document says it happens. Who is involved, which tools they use, where time is lost, which mistakes repeat, and the one number that will tell us whether things improved. We also agree up front what information the system may use and who should see what.

Step 2: Build a focused prototype

Next we build a small version that does the core job on real examples. You see it working, try it, and tell us what's wrong. This is the cheapest point to change direction, and it's where most of the useful learning happens.

Step 3: Connect your tools

An AI system is only useful if it lives where your team works. We connect it to the tools you already have: WhatsApp, your website, a CRM, spreadsheets, an ecommerce store, ad accounts, documents or internal systems. People shouldn't have to change how they work to benefit.

Step 4: Improve with feedback

Real use always surfaces exceptions: unusual customer questions, edge cases, local preferences. We review what the system gets right and wrong, refine it, and let it learn your business reality over time. When the workflow is working well, we decide together whether to extend it or move to the next one.

Most first projects are one of six systems: a WhatsApp lead agent, a sales follow-up system, an AI marketing engine, a customer support agent, catalog and product visuals, or an internal knowledge agent. See all six.

What you don't need

You don't need to understand models or prompts, and you don't need an in-house tech team. You need someone who owns the workflow and can tell us when the output is right. If you'd like to start, email mamta@seemara.com with a short note about your business.

Questions

How long until we see something working?

The aim is a working prototype early in the project, on real examples, before any larger investment.

Do we have to change our tools?

No. Seemara connects the system to the tools you already use, such as WhatsApp, spreadsheets, a CRM or your website.
