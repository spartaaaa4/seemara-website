Aha Frames

Introducing Aha Frames, Seemara's animation and e-learning studio

MM
Mamta MorFounder, Seemara · 24 November 2026 · 5 min read
In short

Aha Frames is a new studio from Seemara, founded by Mamta Mor, the founder of Seemara. It writes, designs and animates 2D and 3D video, e-learning and SCORM courses, and animated ads and reels for learning and marketing teams. You can see its work at ahaframes.com.

Most of the AI systems we build at Seemara exist to help a team explain something: a product to a customer, a process to a new hire, an offer to a buyer scrolling past. Again and again, the clearest way to explain was a short, well-made video. So we built a studio for it. It's called Aha Frames.

What Aha Frames is

Aha Frames is Seemara's animation and e-learning studio. I founded it alongside Seemara, and it shares Seemara's way of working: start with the real problem, show something early, and improve it with feedback. Its line is simple: animation and e-learning that makes it click.

What it makes

  • 2D animation: characters, motion graphics and explainers, drawn in your brand.
  • 3D animation: products, places and equipment with depth and real light.
  • E-learning: lessons with a knowledge check, packaged as SCORM so they run in your LMS.
  • Ads and reels: animated and visual ads, cut for every feed and size.

One team can make a training course, the launch video that announces it, and the reels that promote it, so the message stays consistent everywhere.

How a project runs

A first 2D video takes about two weeks: a brief on day one, the script, then the look, then animation, then delivery. You approve the script and the look before anything is animated, which is where most of the expensive rework in video usually comes from. 3D work and full courses take longer, and the quote says how long.

Aha Frames works in English and ten Indian languages: Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi and Odia, each checked by a native speaker.

Who it's for

Aha Frames is built for learning and development teams and for marketing teams: sales and product training, shop-floor processes and safety, soft skills and leadership programmes, product explainers and launch campaigns. If your team already has slides, manuals, SOPs or recordings, those become the starting material for scripts.

How it fits with the rest of Seemara

Seemara now has three offerings. Custom AI systems take repeated work off a team. Kaizen turns a brand's context into AI ad creatives. And Aha Frames brings studio craft to animation and e-learning. They share one idea: understand the business first, then make something that works for it.

To see samples in every style, visit ahaframes.com. To talk about a project, email mamta@seemara.com.
