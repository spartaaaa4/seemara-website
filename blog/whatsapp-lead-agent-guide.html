What a WhatsApp lead agent does for an Indian SME

MM
Mamta MorFounder, Seemara · 24 November 2026 · 6 min read
In short

A WhatsApp lead agent greets every enquiry, asks the questions your sales team would ask, answers routine questions from your own information, records the lead, and hands serious buyers to a person with the context already collected. It works within WhatsApp's business messaging rules, and it should always offer a way to reach a human.

For many Indian businesses, WhatsApp is the sales channel. That's also why leads slip: messages arrive at night, during busy hours, or to the wrong person, and the follow-up depends on someone remembering. A WhatsApp lead agent fixes the remembering.

What the agent actually does

  1. Replies straight away, any time of day, so the customer isn't left waiting.
  2. Asks qualifying questions: what they need, quantity or budget, location, timeline. The same questions your best salesperson would ask.
  3. Answers the basics from your catalog, price list and policies, and says so plainly when it doesn't know.
  4. Records the lead in a sheet or CRM with everything collected so far.
  5. Hands over to a person when the lead is ready, or when the customer asks for one, with a summary so they don't repeat themselves.
  6. Reminds your team when a promising lead hasn't been followed up.

What it needs to be connected to

  • Your WhatsApp Business number, through the WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • Your product information: catalog, prices, service areas, timings.
  • Somewhere to record leads, such as a Google Sheet or your CRM.
  • The people who take over: who handles which kind of lead.

The rules it has to respect

WhatsApp has clear rules for businesses. Customers should have chosen to message you. Outside the 24-hour window after a customer's last message, a business can only start a conversation with a pre-approved message template. A well-built agent works within these rules rather than around them, and it never pretends to be a person: it says it's an assistant and offers a human.

The most important design choice is the handover. An agent that traps customers in a loop loses sales. One that hands over at the right moment, with context, wins them.

How to tell if it's working

Pick a few numbers before you start: time to first reply, the share of enquiries that get qualified, how many qualified leads get a human follow-up within a day, and how many convert. Compare them with the month before.

Starting small

You don't need every product and every scenario on day one. Start with your most common enquiry type, get the questions and the handover right, then expand. Questions

Can a WhatsApp agent message customers first?

Only within WhatsApp's rules: customers should have opted in, and outside the 24-hour window after their last message a business must use a pre-approved template.

Will customers know they're talking to an AI?

They should. A good agent says it's an assistant and always offers a way to reach a person.
